PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy explains how Fixr collects and uses information submitted through the Fixr website.

Last updated: September 25, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you join the Fixr waitlist, we collect the information you choose to provide through the waitlist form.

This currently includes:

  • Your email address
  • Your name, if you choose to provide it
  • Whether you are joining the waitlist as a Customer or Service Provider

2. How We Use Your Information

We use waitlist information to maintain the Fixr pre-launch waitlist, understand interest from potential customers and service providers, and communicate with people who have asked to receive information about Fixr's launch.

3. Sharing Your Information

Fixr does not sell your waitlist information. Information may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect Fixr, its users, or others.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through the Fixr website. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Your Choices

You may ask us to update or remove the personal information you submitted through the Fixr waitlist by contacting us at fixr.beta@gmail.com.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Fixr develops and its website and services change. When the policy is updated, the date shown at the top of this page will be revised.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or information you submitted through the Fixr website, contact us at fixr.beta@gmail.com.
