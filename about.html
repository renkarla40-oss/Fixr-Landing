ABOUT FIXR

Local services.
A better way.

Fixr is being built to make finding and working with local service providers simpler, more organized and more connected.

WHY FIXR

Getting things done shouldn't feel complicated.

Finding someone for a repair, cleaning job, electrical work, plumbing or another home service often comes down to asking around, searching through scattered listings or relying on word of mouth.

Fixr is being built to bring that experience into one place, giving customers a simpler way to find local service providers and giving skilled providers another way to connect with people who need their services.

BUILT FOR TRINIDAD & TOBAGO

Built around the way local services actually work.

Fixr is being developed with Trinidad & Tobago at its core, creating a more organized way for customers and local service providers to connect, communicate and manage the job from request to completion.
